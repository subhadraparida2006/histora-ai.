# Problem Statement

- History students read very long books, PDFs and old sources
- Old texts in Latin, Persian and Sanskrit are hard to understand
- Too many dates, people and events are hard to remember
- No single portal to summarize, translate, build timelines and quiz

edunetfoundation

# Project Workflow

## Input

Upload or paste a PDF, notes or an old text

## Processing

AI summarizes, translates and extracts dates

## Output

Summary, timeline and quiz

- **Tools used:** HTML5, internal CSS, simple JavaScript
- **AI tools:** ChatGPT for prompts and IBM Bob for code

edunetfoundation

# Key Features

## Source Summarizer

One-page summary with dates, people and causes

## Ask The Source

Ask questions about any document

## Timeline Builder

Automatic timeline from your notes

## Old Text Translator

Latin, Persian and Sanskrit to simple English

## Compare Sources

Agreement, disagreement and bias

## AI History Tutor

Quiz, flashcards and essay outline
